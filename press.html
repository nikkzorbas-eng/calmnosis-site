The Book You Can Hear

In Henderson, Nevada, Nikkos Zorbas has spent five years turning long-form music into paintings. His new coffee-table book lets you listen to both.

CALMNOSIS — The Art of Sound, by Nikkos Zorbas

Henderson, Nev. — Most art books are silent. This one is not.

CALMNOSIS — The Art of Sound, the new coffee-table book by Henderson-based artist Nikkos Zorbas, presents 25 works of gallery fine art across 76 pages. Beneath each major plate sits a code the reader scans with a phone, and the painting begins to play. Every image in the book is also a piece of music — a full recording, heard exactly as its composer made it. The book’s back cover states the premise plainly: “Every picture in this book is a recording you can hear.”

The music is NEUROJAZZ, the sound Zorbas has developed under the name CALMNOSIS: long-form instrumental sessions, most running about forty minutes, composed for deep focus and unhurried listening. It has found its audience quietly and at scale. The CALMNOSIS channel on YouTube has grown to more than 12,000 subscribers and over 1.6 million views. One session, “Feel the Sound,” has been heard more than 437,000 times. Another, “SYNAPTIC FLOW,” reached 93,000 views in its first seventeen days — an unusual pace for music that asks for forty minutes of a listener’s attention rather than thirty seconds.

Zorbas arrived at this sound by a long road. Recording as Nikkos, he released the 12-inch vinyl single “Don’t Fear The Rave,” produced with Roy Hay of Culture Club and Hein Hoven. He later served as a voting member of the Recording Academy, which presents the GRAMMY Awards. After years away from releasing music, he returned in January 2021 with TOUCH, the first CALMNOSIS album, and began building the catalogue that now fills the book — sessions, short wordless films, and song films, all made from his home base in Henderson, in the Las Vegas valley.

The book is organized the way the catalogue grew. A trilogy of sphere works — “SPHERICAL HARMONY,” “SPHEROSOPHY,” and “SYNAPTIC FLOW” — anchors the collection, paintings of turning orbs and currents of light that correspond to three of the most-heard sessions. Films sit alongside them as stills and stories. Each plate carries a short line of text, and the writing is restrained throughout. The opening session is introduced with a single sentence: “Before there was light, there was a tone.” Of another work, Zorbas writes, “Seduction begins the moment the sound touches you.” The effect is less like reading a book about music than like walking a quiet gallery in which every room is audible.

The square 8.5-by-8.5-inch format keeps the paintings at the center. Nothing in the book explains how the music is made, and Zorbas prefers it that way. The work, he suggests, should be met the way a listener meets it — directly, without footnotes.

CALMNOSIS — The Art of Sound is available now on Amazon in paperback ($34.99) and as a Kindle eBook ($9.99). Limited-edition framed prints of the session art are available through CALMNOSIS at calmnosis.com, where the shop is linked.

Zorbas is also the author of Intelligence Under Pressure, a book on maintaining clarity and performance when it matters most, available on Amazon in Kindle and paperback editions.
